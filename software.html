Risk Management

Engineering is not only about building systems — it is also about anticipating problems and designing reliable solutions.

During the development of SkySense, several potential risks were identified and carefully considered in order to ensure the reliability and success of the mission.

⚙️ Technical Challenges ⏳ Time Management 🔌 System Integration 📚 Learning New Technologies

One of the main risks involved technical difficulties related to the implementation of new components and technologies. Since some systems required learning unfamiliar architectures and communication protocols, unexpected challenges could potentially slow development. To reduce this risk, the team performed preliminary research, tested components individually and consulted official documentation before integration.
Time management also represented an important challenge. Certain tasks required more time than initially expected, potentially affecting later stages of development. To mitigate this risk, the team organized the workflow into structured phases and regularly monitored progress to adapt schedules when necessary.
Another possible issue involved system integration. Even when individual components worked correctly on their own, compatibility problems could appear once all systems were connected together. For this reason, incremental testing was carried out throughout the development process, allowing issues to be identified and solved early.
Finally, some team members initially had limited experience with specific technologies used in the project. This challenge was addressed through collaborative learning, tutorials, experimentation and continuous knowledge sharing within the team.


Launch Risks

🐞 Software Bugs 📡 Communication Failures ⚡ Performance Limitations 🚀 Deployment Issues

During the launch phase, additional risks were considered regarding the operation of the CanSat under real mission conditions.
One possible risk involved undetected software bugs or unexpected system errors that could appear during flight. To minimize this possibility, the team performed repeated testing sessions and verified the stability of the most critical software functions before deployment.
Another concern was related to communication reliability and system performance. The CanSat must continuously transmit environmental data to the ground station while also storing data locally on the SD card. To ensure redundancy and reliability, local storage was implemented as a backup solution in case of temporary transmission issues.
Finally, technical problems during deployment or operation could occur, including unexpected hardware behavior or configuration issues. To reduce the impact of these problems, backup configurations and recovery procedures were prepared in advance, allowing the system to be quickly restored if necessary.
